HOW TO PREPARE

Understand the process before you apply.

Prepare a clear description of your organization, the project you want to fund, the expected outcomes and a realistic budget. Confirm the program's eligibility requirements and application deadline using its official materials.

Never pay an individual or unverified third party to secure a grant. Legitimate program fees, if any, should be disclosed in formal terms and independently verifiable.
